A measured field of nested squares, interrupted by one diagonal break and a small white center.

The first pass sets the outer rhythm: a square inside a square, each edge held
at a slightly different value. The interruption is deliberately small, but it
changes how the eye reads the whole field.

The second and third studies are alternate crops and reversals from the same
rule set. They are kept together here so students can compare a system with
its variations instead of treating each export as an isolated image.
