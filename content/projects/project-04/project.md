Botanical fragments translated into a narrow vocabulary of blocks, voids, and silver-gray edges.

The source image began as a walk through a greenhouse after closing time. I
was less interested in naming the plant than in recording the way a leaf turns
from surface into silhouette when the available light is reduced.

The bitmap is intentionally restrained: a few values carry the stem, the
fold, and the empty air around them. Compression becomes part of the drawing,
not a defect to hide.

For a class exercise, ask what can be removed before the fragment stops
reading as a living thing. The useful constraint is to keep the gesture while
discarding the description.
